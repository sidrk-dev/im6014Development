## What was validated

The checker loads the canonical [SA01 contract](../../../docs/single-arm-contract.json) and validates its embodiment with the installed Dream SDK:

- One arm part with public `state.arm` and `action.arm` width 3.
- Registration maps the public part to the internal `right_arm` training slot.
- One `overhead` video stream; no head camera.
- Joint-action training with `use_eef=False`.
- Joint metadata passes `embodiments.check_joints`.
- A mocked joint-action exchange returns a two-row, three-wide `action.right_arm` chunk. The public reply exposes `per_key.action.arm`.
- The wire observation uses `state.right_arm` and `video.overhead`, not `state.arm`, and contains no left-arm or gripper keys.
- A two-wide state is rejected before transport.
- A two-wide action response is rejected.
- No hosted model, cloud call, or motor call occurs.

The existing sample `verify_dream.py` validator was also rerun as a regression check. Its verdict and key fields match the published RC00 [SDK report](../../rolling-contact/dream_contract.json), including the two-coordinate sample, mocked transport, and no hosted calls.
