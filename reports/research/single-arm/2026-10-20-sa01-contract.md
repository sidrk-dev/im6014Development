## What was frozen

- Selected profile: `sim_prototype_3`, the three-coordinate CAD prototype. It is separate from `sim_final_rolling` (RC01) and from `sim_rolling_sample` (the two-coordinate public example).
- Joint order: `J1` = `revolute_2` base, motor 0; `J2` = `revolute_1` shoulder, motor 1; `J3` = `revolute_3` elbow, motor 2.
- Units: radians. State and action are absolute joint targets, in that order.
- Policy interface: `state.arm` and `per_key.action.arm`, width 3. The client may map the public part to its internal right-arm training slot, but that is not a left-arm channel.
- Observation: one overhead RGB camera at 320x240 JPEG, 30 Hz, one frame of history.
- Timing: 100 Hz control, 30 Hz policy/camera/dataset, 250 ms maximum observation age, one-row action chunk, one-row execution prefix, 30 s simulation timeout.
- Task: fixed-tool reaching of a visible red marker. Success is at most 20 mm from the frozen tool reference point for 1.0 s, without human intervention.
- Budget: 20 successful training episodes and 5 validation episodes; 10 held-out evaluation scenes (6 static, 4 shifted); at least 8 successes including 3 shifted.
- Excluded from the policy: passive coordinates, left-arm channels, gripper channels, wrist cameras, EEF actions, evaluator coordinates, and scripted task paths.

## Deliberately unresolved

- The exact tool reference frame is not present in the CAD asset. SA03 must freeze it; do not treat `part_1_4` or any other provisional link as accepted.
- The overhead camera pose/extrinsics and marker diameter are not frozen. SA03 owns both. Do not invent a marker size from the image.
- `J2` and `J3` are continuous CAD joints. Physical travel and simulation envelopes remain uncommissioned.
- Prototype physics, inertias, collisions, and controller behavior are not validated. The contract does not claim otherwise.
- `sim_final_rolling` geometry, independent coordinates, and drive relation belong to RC01.
- Scene sets and the held-out evaluation set are frozen by SA05 and SA10 before training.

## Verification

Run:

```sh
python3 scripts/check_single_arm_contract.py \
  --prototype-dir .worktrees/controller-c07-c10/arm_assets \
  --report reports/research/single-arm/2026-10-20-contract-check.json
```

The checker validates the contract invariants, the generated reference page, the prototype hashes, and the three-joint order. `--write-doc` regenerates the Markdown page after a contract change.

The machine-readable result is [2026-10-20-contract-check.json](2026-10-20-contract-check.json). A contract-only run without `--prototype-dir` does not verify the external asset.
